Received a **Merit Award** at the **Bangladesh ICT and Innovation Awards 2026**, organised by the Bangladesh ICT and Innovation Network (BIIN), for our solution **CiviLedger**.

- **355** project submissions from across Bangladesh
- **118** shortlisted for final evaluation
- **44** projects honoured, CiviLedger among them
- Judged by a 40-member jury from industry, academia, government, banking and the startup ecosystem

The award ceremony was held on 3 October 2026 at BRAC University, Dhaka.
